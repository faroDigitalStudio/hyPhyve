The Big Picture

A kingdom that keeps ecosystems working.

Fungi are neither plants nor animals. They recycle nutrients, form partnerships with other organisms, and help shape the environments we depend on.

We turn this overlooked science into clear, memorable learning experiences, connecting microscopic details to the bigger picture.

What We Explore

From spores to ecosystems and beyond.

Fungal science connects the microscopic and the monumental. These four themes provide a starting point for investigation, demonstrations, and discussion.

Fungal Biology

How spores become mycelium, why mushrooms fruit, and what makes fungi a kingdom of their own.

Cultivation & Labs

Agar cultures, sterile technique, substrates, growing conditions, and the journey from culture to harvest.

Fungal Ecology

Decomposition, nutrient cycling, mycorrhizal partnerships, and fungi's role in healthy ecosystems.

Emerging Uses

Food and fermentation, mycelium-based materials, environmental applications, and open research questions.
